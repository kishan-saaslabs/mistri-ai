Features

Everything you need to read the room

From raw recording to ranked pipeline in minutes — every claim traceable to the transcript.

Automatic call scoring

Every call gets a health score and deal signal the moment it’s processed — no manual review.

Evidence-linked insights

Every insight links back to the exact quote and timestamp. Open the receipt in one click.

Ask across your pipeline

Ask a plain-English question about any deal, rep, or call and get an answer with sources.

Deals & team organization

Group calls under deals, invite teammates, and control who has access to what.

Upload or link recordings

Drop an MP3/MP4 or paste a recording URL — Mistri transcribes and maps it to the deal.

Open-source & self-hostable

MIT-licensed and runs on your own infrastructure. No black boxes, no vendor lock-in.

How it works

Three steps from call to clarity

1

Bring in your calls

Upload a recording or paste a link. Mistri transcribes it with speaker diarization and maps it to a deal.

2

Mistri scores & extracts

Deal health, buying signals, risks, and next steps are pulled out automatically — each tied to the transcript.

3

Ask & act

Review the ranked pipeline, ask questions in plain English, and move on the deals that matter.
