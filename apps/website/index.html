Features

Everything you need to read the room

From raw recording to organized pipeline — every claim traceable to the transcript line it came from.

Call insights, drafted for you

Summary, objections, customer wants, next steps, and a follow-up email draft — extracted from every call automatically.

Evidence-linked, always

Every insight cites the exact transcript segment it came from — answers are checked against the source before they're shown.

Ask Mistri

Ask a plain-English question about any deal or call. Answers are grounded in your transcripts, with citations back to the source.

Deals & team organization

Group calls under deals, invite teammates, and control who has access to what.

Upload or link recordings

Drop an MP3/MP4 or paste a recording URL — Mistri transcribes and maps it to the deal.

Open-source & self-hostable

MIT-licensed and runs on your own infrastructure. No black boxes, no vendor lock-in.

How it works

Three steps from call to clarity

1

Bring in your calls

Upload a recording or paste a link. Mistri transcribes it with speaker diarization and maps it to a deal.

2

Mistri extracts the takeaways

Summary, objections, customer wants, next steps, and a follow-up email draft — pulled out automatically, each tied to the transcript.

3

Ask & act

Browse your deals, ask Mistri questions in plain English, and move on what matters — backed by citations.

Open source
